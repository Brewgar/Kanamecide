# Current Position — chief-architect

## Overall Assessment

The Kanamecide research organization has now passed through two rounds of infrastructure
hardening, and today (2026-10-02) has its first normative description of the derived
layer (`research/INFRASTRUCTURE.md`) plus two advisory surfaces (`freshness`,
`novelty`) that project the existing memory discipline onto new record kinds. The core
assertion this seat has earned — and verified three times — is that the measurably good
part of this project is its claim discipline: hypothesis → pre-registered experiment →
verification review → decision. Phase-2 classical search is measured. What remains
unproven is the entire premise of the research objective: that the current engine's
evaluation can train away the handle of hand-tuned terms and start climbing to
state-of-the-art. Ready-check for the first training experiment (E-0013) is not a
code-readiness problem but a process-readiness one (leakage contract, dedup key,
scope ruling, and the F-U14 tactical suite) that the record chain names through
R-0019..R-0026, S-0034..S-0042, and this session's closure of that chain.

## Strongest Parts

- A self-checking memory: `research.py validate`, `imem.py lint`, the corpus invariants,
  plus `promotion` rungs with receipt links that anyone can re-derive.
- Verified protocols (E-0011 dataset, E-0012 harness, the F-0002 recovery) that each
  carry their own pre-registered gates and their own independent verification chains.
- A standing line of measured limitations: privacy of search (PEXT ceiling), data
  calibration (LOSS-margin unwinnability vs the measured achievable gain), and the
  fitting-risk ladder (F-U1..F-U14).
- The platform separation of record from derived state — the `records are the truth`
  invariant that the layer has now learned to prove at every read.

## Weakest Parts (from my seat)

- Too much evidence lives outside the protected record ring (`m0_audit`, `build`, the
  context packs). Records reliably recover what they say; a reader that skips the
  referenced side evidence still loses the session's derivation path.
- Rounds carry long-running claims that look done but are not: W-0003, W-0006, and HO-0005
  each have a provenance-resolution half-life, and the freshness report exists because
  today they are *visible* as stale, not resolved.

## Current Preferred Architecture (research-system side)

- Canonical Markdown store (+ git); derived SQLite/JSON layer rebuilt on demand;
  promoted tiers of belief backed by measured experiments; claim/finding ledgers whose
  terms are enforce; verification as an owned+re-runnable seat role; and the PROCESS
  loop (observe → question → experiment → review → decide → record) that now runs under
  the same gates the engine's runs run under.

## Highest-Value Research Direction

A measured confirmation that the evaluation can learn past hand-tuned terms at the
current data yield (the E-0013 family after F-U14). Everything in this sitting is
measurably blocked on that (Q-0006, Q-0008, and the training-readiness gates), and the
system today gives the open questions their own evidence, their own priorities, and
their own reviewers.

## Major Concerns

- Bureaucracy is not zero-variance: the promotion ladder is honest only if the
  strongest experiments survive independent verification; the layer's `promote` tool
  reports what the inputs imply, and the inputs must stay measured.
- A single-seat close on a governed finding (FND-0032) has been corrected by routing
  (HO-0021); that pattern exists because the seat model depends on verification being
  assigned *to a different seat* every time it matters.

## Open Questions

- Does the data the project has (E-0011 games) carry the information the hypothesis
  H-0013 expects its fit to extract (R-0019/Q2's unattributable-gain warning)?
- Can the current toolchain (local CPU + the learning engine's own instrumentation)
  keep the experiment loop at measurable speed under real conditions?
- Will the layer's semantics (direction, parameter, scope) let a later agent catch
  an agent that claims strength without an experiment — as seen in several rounds?
